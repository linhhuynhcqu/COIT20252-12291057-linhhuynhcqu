Process analysis identifies and examines all process-related activities and measures the operations and effectiveness of those activities in meeting the organisation’s goals
A process is a defined set of sequential or parallel activities that achieve a particular, definable, measurable result


Process analysis is one of the important stage or path to continue monitoring or event trigger such as strategic planning, implement new product, find problem for performance, regulatory Requirements.
. If you want to changing process, you need to understand what this happening, why this happening and where are the problems.Without the analysis step, company maybe solve wrong problems so that why we should understand and analyse issues, tackle risks.

Process analysis can be applied to address current and future improvement opportunities, which may include:
More efficient operations
Avoidance and/or mitigation of risks
Potential blended operations between two organizations
Impact analysis of pending federal or state regulations

**Why do process analysis **
Evaluate how efficiently the organisation is working to meet its objectives
To get a shared understanding of how the work gets done currently
Help redesign processes to better meet the goals of the organisation
Identify obsolete processes
Make informed decisions about how to adapt to a changing environment

Need to capture the customer behaviour => improve service, not out of time and enhance customer experience.


Information form process analysis
Clear statement of the strategy, goals, and objectives of the organisation
Description of the business environment and context of the process (why the process exists)
View of the process within a larger cross-functional process

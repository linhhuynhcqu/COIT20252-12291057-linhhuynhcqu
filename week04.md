## Introduction to Process Analysis
_**Artefact:**_ Webpage Article - What is process analysis? (IBM 2026)

Process analysis includes the examination of each step in a organisation process to determine how it identify areas, operates for change, and ensure it aligns with business goals. The IBM article shows the way analysing activities helps company reduces mistakes, adapt to changing environment and improve output. Moreover, process analysis is closely linked to Business Process Management (BPM), showing how structured analysis supports decision-making (IBM 2026).

The artefact presents description of value and purpose of process analysis. I can understand why analysing workflow is crucial before making any changes, particularly to avoid tackling the wrong issues or ignoring hidden challenges. In addition, it shows how process analysis improves system optimisation and provides a basis for effective BPM.

Businesses frequently use different analysis techniques to detect problems and offer improvement opportunities. This video introduces three methods for process analysis: the 5 Whys, Pareto Analysis, and Fishbone Diagram. The author discusses how the 5 Whys uncovers basic problems through repeated questioning, how the Fishbone Diagram clearly outlines potential causes, and how Pareto charts assist prioritise issues by frequency or impact (Zaidan Consulting 2025).

This video displays practical tools that support effective process evaluation. Rather than focusing on symptoms, each methods assists identify the root cause of an issue. Moreover, using various techniques may support BPM reduce risk of assumptions by reducing errors, improving accuracy, and guiding better choices.


## Real-world Example of Process Analysis  

_**Artefact:**_ Webpage Article - Data Science at Netflix: How Advanced Data & Analytics Helps Netflix Generate Billions (Mixson 2025)

The following article highlights how Netflix uses advanced data analytics to assess and optimise systems across user engagement, content delivery, and production strategy. With over 203 million customers, Netflix tracks detailed user behaviour such as device type, search activity, viewing time, pause and replay actions. Its suggestion engine utilises this information to priorities video most likely to be watched, leading to 80% of streamed content coming from recommendations. In addition, the article provides how Netflix predicts the popular of original content through analysing user touchpoint and audience demographic (Mixson 2025).

Mixson (2025) shares a strong real world example of how process analysis enhances customer satisfaction and organisation performance. As a result, Netflix’s apply process analysis to demonstrates actions and procedures improve decisions-making and performance in digital environments.

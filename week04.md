## Introduction to Process Analysis (125 words)
Artefact: Journal Article - What is process analysis? (IBM 2026)

Process analysis is a method of detailing and examining the steps involved in a process to understand how it works, identify areas for improvement, and help ensure that it meets its objectives efficiently and effectively (IBM 2026). It helps organisations improve workflows, reduce errors, adapt to changing environments and meet with business goals. This article clearly explains the purpose and value of process analysis in a way that is easy to understand and also mention about the relationship with BPM. It helped me see why analysing each step of a process is essential before making any changes, especially to avoid solving the wrong problems or missing hidden bottlenecks. The article strengthened my understanding of how process analysis improves workflows, supports business goals, and encourages continuous improvement. It also showed me how better resource allocation and more informed decision making come from analysing processes.
